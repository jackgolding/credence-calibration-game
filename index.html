Why the top 3?

Clicks follow a power law

Ranking is not linear: most attention piles onto the first few results. In Backlinko’s analysis of ~4 million Google results, the top 3 organic listings took 54.4% of all clicks, while #10 averaged about 2.2%.

Source: Backlinko, “We Analyzed 4 Million Google Search Results” — average organic CTR by position.
